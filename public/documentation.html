01Game Lore & Core Concepts

The premise: a salvage economy in Sol

Station Agora is a multi-agent trading game set in the Sol system (theme: The Atlas Problem). Four corporate "fleets" haul and trade scrap, ore, food, fuel and machinery between four stations, pay for everything in CR (Credits), and are ranked by net worth. Every rule below is enforced by one referee process (agora/referee.py) over a double-entry ledger; the HTTP API lives in agora/server.py.

  • Prices move on a mean-reverting random walk per station and good (agora/spatial.py), nudged by GalNet news, and quoted at each station by NPC depots plus the fleets' own resting orders.
  • Arbitrage is physical. Goods are bought and sold only by a ship docked at the order's station, and moving between stations burns FUEL and takes whole rounds.
  • Everything else is optional pressure. Contracts, debt, takeovers, piracy, sabotage, lobbying and stock short-selling are layered on top (see the Mechanics Catalog). On the live server almost all of them default to ON (build_referee_from_env in agora/server.py; a bare AgoraReferee() used in tests is conservative and leaves most of them off).
Heads-up: the older single-station, FRAG-and-CR framing (docs/wire-spec.md) and the "two commodities" framing in docs/game-guide.md are out of date. The code trades five goods across four stations.

The canonical goods are COMMODITIES = ["FRAG", "FUEL", "FOOD", "ORE", "MACHINERY"] (agora/spatial.py). All quantities and limit prices are integers. FUEL is a normal order-book good and the propellant a ship burns when it moves.

  • Base volumes: there is no per-commodity base-volume constant in the code. The nearest equivalents are the depot seed (100,000 units of each good and 1,000,000 CR per depot) and the reactive depot shelf and hold sizes (2,000-unit capacity, see Depots).
  • Aliases (COMMODITY_ALIASES, applied by normalize_commodity to every order and transit): ORGANICS to FOOD, BANANA to FRAG, PARTS to MACHINERY, TECH to MACHINERY. Matching is case-insensitive.
  • Perishables: PERISHABLE_COMMODITIES = {"FOOD", "ORGANICS"}. A FOOD shipment defaults to perishable; the transit request's perishable flag can override it either way for any good. Decay is non-zero only on belt routes: at arrival the ship loses floor(qty * 0.05 * transit_rounds) units, where transit_rounds = max(1, arrival_round - departure_round) (so storm delays add decay). Perishable goods that sit unmanifested in the ship's hold decay by the same formula when the ship lands on a belt route (#282).
  • Other tradeable instruments: the four fleet stocks EQ_AMOS, EQ_MARV, EQ_ZERO, EQ_AERL trade on the Ceres book only (see Fleets). The legacy symbol BANANA still has an (unused) book per station but is normalized to FRAG before any order is checked.
  • Spot price engine (StationPriceEngine): new = old + theta*(base - old) + shock_nudge + momentum + inventory + flow + noise, floored at 1.0 and rounded to 2 decimals. Defaults: theta = 0.15, noise vol = 0.8 (Gaussian), seed 42 for a bare engine. GalNet shock nudge is drift_bias * base * 0.5. The momentum, inventory-sensitivity and flow terms default to 0 and are tuned by AGORA_GOODS_MOMENTUM, AGORA_GOODS_INVENTORY_SENSITIVITY, AGORA_GOODS_FLOW_SENSITIVITY, AGORA_GOODS_DELIVERY_SCALE (default 1000).
Gotcha: FUEL is the one good the leaderboard never values, and the one good the corporate distress sale never sells (agora/corporate.py sells FRAG, FOOD, ORE and MACHINERY only). Treat it as working capital, not as a store of value.

CR, the SYSTEM account, and the ledger

  • CR is the only currency and the numeraire. Balances are integers.
  • SYSTEM is the issuer and sink. It is the one account allowed to go negative: genesis debits it for everything minted, and tolls, fuel burn, fines, fees, bonds, upgrades and ship purchases are paid to it. Because every transaction sums to zero, the sum of all balances including SYSTEM is always zero. Every other account must stay at or above zero (verify_ledger_invariants in agora/referee.py checks conservation, non-negativity, ledger-to-account reconciliation, goods-on-ships, one-trip-per-ship and hold limits).
  • Escrow lives in SYSTEM. Cargo in flight, peer-trade goods and CR, tender-offer and loan-offer principal, and short-sale collateral are all held on SYSTEM until released.
  • Depot accounts (depot_earth, depot_luna, depot_mars, depot_ceres) and the stock-exchange market maker (depot_exchange) are NPC accounts. The depot_ prefix keeps them off the leaderboard.

Fleet stock (equity)

  • Each fleet issues 1,000 shares (FLEET_EQUITIES in agora/equity.py). Genesis shares are minted from SYSTEM.
  • Live defaults: every fleet starts with AGORA_RIVAL_SHARES = 100 of each rival's stock, and the exchange market maker holds AGORA_EXCHANGE_SHARES = 100 of each fleet (capped at 200). The issuer keeps the rest, which is 600 of its own 1,000 at default settings. With both set to 0 an issuer holds everything.
  • The exchange (agora/exchange.py, account depot_exchange) quotes every stock two-sided on the Ceres book each round around a reference price that reverts to a smoothed NAV: spread DEFAULT_SPREAD = 0.03, depth from 20 up to 80 shares a side by recent volume, seed cash 100,000 CR, volatility AGORA_EXCHANGE_VOL = 0.12 live. Public events nudge a stock (for example +2% on a shipyard upgrade, -8% when a sabotage or privateer contract is exposed).
  • Rival shares count in net worth at their mark; a fleet's own shares do not.

02Gameplay Loop & Turn Lifecycle

The loop in one paragraph

Time in Station Agora is a discrete round counter owned by the referee (AgoraReferee.current_round, agora/referee.py). Between rounds, fleets act freely and concurrently: they read the market, place and cancel limit orders, start trips, and use the optional mechanics. A round "steps" when something calls step_round(): prices move, NPC depots requote, ships land, contracts, debts and borrow fees settle. Then fleets see the new world and act again. Standings are mark-to-market net worth, and for a burst run they are ranked by how much net worth changed since the burst began.

Not wall-clock, not Discord. The old "five-minute bell" in docs/game-guide.md is a Discord-bot convention. The game clock is the referee's own round counter, driven by the background ticker (default one round every 60 seconds), by admin bursts, or by a direct call to POST /stations/step_round.

Ticker status, pause, and the inactivity watchdog

  • Watchdog. After every ticked round the ticker checks whether referee.current_seq (the monotonic event sequence) advanced. If not, the round was "quiet". After inactivity_rounds consecutive quiet rounds (default DEFAULT_INACTIVITY_ROUNDS = 2880, which is 48 hours at 60 s) the ticker pauses itself with reason inactivity_watchdog: .... Any order, fill or trip resets the count. This keeps an idle table from burning compute.
  • Durable state. Desired state (running, paused, stopped), the quiet-round count, and a lease (LEASE_DURATION_SEC = 180.0) are persisted each tick to ticker_state. On boot the ticker resumes from that state: a manual or watchdog pause survives a restart (the engine starts, then pauses with restored_from_persisted_state:<state>); a run resumes with its quiet count intact.
  • Controls (any valid bearer token, not only admin, in the current code): POST /referee/admin/ticker/pause, /ticker/resume, /ticker/config (interval_sec, inactivity_rounds). Read-only: GET /referee/ticker/status returns running, paused, pause_reason, interval_sec, quiet_round_count, current_round, next_tick_eta_sec and the burst fields.
  • Round events. The WebSocket terminal stream (/ws/terminal, agora/websocket.py) emits a round message whenever current_round or the floor state changes.

  • Start. The continuous ticker is paused (reason burst:<burst_id>); it resumes after the burst only if it was running when the burst began. The burst id is burst-<unix seconds>-<6 hex>. A burst event (phase: start) is written to the event log.
  • Baseline snapshot. Before the first round, record_burst_baseline stores every fleet's current net worth in burst_baseline (replacing any earlier burst's baseline). If the snapshot fails the burst still runs and standings fall back to absolute net worth.
  • Rounds. A thread waits interval_sec, calls step_round(), writes a tick event with round_index and rounds_remaining, and repeats. A round that raises is logged as a tick with an error and the burst continues.
  • End round. The ticker records burst_end_round = current round + rounds when the burst starts; referee.get_burst_info() exposes active, burst_id, rounds_remaining, rounds_total and end_round, which drive the transit burst-end warning and the briefing line.

  • Escape hatches. POST /referee/admin/burst/cancel (or /burst/stop) stops it early ({"force": true} also clears state immediately); POST /referee/admin/burst/reset force-clears a stuck burst.

What happens inside one round

Order of operations in AgoraReferee.step_round (the round that is ending is R, the new one is R+1):

  • Idle fees for round R are charged (see Idle fee), then ship upkeep (300 CR per ship beyond the first).
  • NPC order flow fills fleet orders resting from round R against the old depot quotes (Order flow).
  • current_round becomes R+1.
  • GalNet steps only if AGORA_GALNET_AUTO_STEP is on (default off on the live server).
  • Prices step (mean reversion, news drift, noise) and are stored; a price_history row is written per station and good.
  • Depots requote (_refresh_depot_orders_locked) and the stock exchange requotes its two-sided stock prices.
  • Piracy: unanswered pirate demands are fought now, before arrivals.
  • Arrivals: every trip with arrival_round <= R+1 lands. Cargo is released from SYSTEM escrow into the ship's hold (net of perishable decay), any docking tariff is charged, the ship docks, and a transit_arrived event is written.
  • Desks: peer-trade escrow, contracts, corporate (debt, distress, bankruptcy, takeovers, negligence, dividends), upgrade unlock news, events (secret leaks, 20% stake disclosures), then standing.
  • Outside the main transaction: equity borrow fees and margin checks, then circuit-breaker reopen auctions for halts that are due. Fog snapshots are recorded last.

The call returns a summary dict (round, prices, arrived_transits, idle_fees, ship_upkeep, circuit_breaker_reopens, and one report per desk).

Trips and the transit lifecycle

POST /stations/transit {destination, commodity?, cargo_qty?, perishable?, escort?, vessel_id?, dry_run?} starts one ship's trip (with dry_run: true it only quotes it, see the quote endpoint). Checks, in order: fleet not out, valid destination, ship resolves and is docked (already_in_transit, invalid_vessel), not already at the destination (invalid_transit), route exists (invalid_route), enough FUEL after committed asks (insufficient_fuel), toll affordable (insufficient_credits_for_toll), cargo available (insufficient_cargo), and if the cargo is FUEL then burn plus cargo must fit in the FUEL balance. Then:

  • The route is priced for the departure round (alignment windows apply). Engine upgrades and any negligence discount reduce the fuel burn (see Mechanics).
  • All of that ship's resting orders at its origin are cancelled, asks included (stock orders stay up). A move needs no cargo_qty: the committed goods are released and ride in the hold.
  • Whole-hold valuation (#282). Every non-CR, non-FUEL good in the ship's hold (hold_cargo), not only the manifest, is valued for the piracy raid odds, any pirate demand and the hazard loss roll. Raids and breaches take manifested cargo first, then the hold. The receipt carries hold_cargo and total_cargo_value.
  • FUEL burn goes ship to SYSTEM; the toll (25 CR on belt routes) goes fleet to SYSTEM; cargo moves ship to SYSTEM escrow.
  • Hazards (delay, loss) and the piracy raid roll are resolved at departure and reported in the response, so the fleet can react.
  • arrival_round = departure_round + route rounds - engine cut + hazard delay. The receipt and the transit book event report this as rounds_duration = arrival_round - departure_round, split into base_rounds (route rounds after the engine cut) and delay_rounds (hazard delay). The ship is in_transit and cannot trade commodities until it lands.
  • Burst-end warning (#289). While a burst runs, a trip that lands at or after the burst's final round gets arrives_after_burst_end: true (top level and in payload) and a warning string on the receipt, because cargo cannot be sold once the burst concludes. may_arrive_after_burst_end (#300) is true whenever the trip is certain to land late or, on a quote, only could if a hazard delay hits (arrival_round_min before the final round but arrival_round_max at or after it); on a receipt, where the delay is already rolled, it equals arrives_after_burst_end. The quote warns in both cases.

Pre-trip quote (#285, #288). dry_run: true (or GET /stations/transit/quote) runs every check above except that it never spends, escrows, cancels or rolls anything, and returns a transit_quote with the arrival window (arrival_round_min to arrival_round_max), the costs, the piracy odds breakdown and the hazard odds. Use it to price a trip before committing.

Cargo in flight is still the fleet's: it counts toward net worth at its origin price, less projected decay.

Quoting and order validation

Orders go to POST /referee/orders (full envelope) or POST /referee/quick_order (flat JSON; buy/bid become a bid, anything else an ask; price/quantity/commodity/station/vessel aliases accepted). A token other than admin or combine must match the payload agent_id, otherwise HTTP 403 unauthorized. Rejects return HTTP 400 with kind: "reject" and a reason.

  • Matching (agora/order_book.py) is price then time priority. A trade executes at the resting order's limit price, so a crossing order can get a better price than its limit but never a worse one. Partial fills rest the remainder. There is no tick size, no lot size and no self-trade prevention in the code.
  • Cancel: POST /referee/orders/cancel (reason order_not_cancellable if unknown or finished) and /orders/cancel_all.
  • Circuit-breaker interaction. A commodity order that would cross a resting order priced outside the station's band is accepted and rested, and trading in that book halts (see Circuit breakers).
  • Resting orders commit capital. A resting bid ties up qty * limit_price CR and a resting ask ties up qty goods until filled or cancelled. A departure cancels that ship's orders at its origin.
seq_seen is advisory only. Every order carries seq_seen (the event sequence the agent last saw) and the referee stores it on the order, but nothing compares it with the current sequence: there is no staleness rejection and no repricing. A stale order matches by exactly the same price-time rule as a fresh one. docs/wire-spec.md, which says stale orders fill "at current market price" with slippage, does not describe the code.
quick_order differs. It reads commodity (default FRAG) when instrument is absent.
Idempotency and ids. Reuse the same order_id and terms to retry safely. Reusing an id with different terms is rejected, not overwritten.

Net worth: how fleets are marked

get_leaderboard() in agora/referee.py computes one row per fleet:

  • liquid CR, plus CR held in peer-trade escrow and contract bonds;
  • plus docked cargo valued at the spot price of the station each ship or hold sits at, goods in peer escrow at their escrow station, and cargo in flight (net of projected decay) at its origin station's spot;
  • plus fitted upgrades and bought ships, each at 50% of price;
  • with corporate on: plus tender-offer escrow, loan-offer escrow and receivables on active loans, minus payables on active loans and referee debt;
  • plus rival stock at its mark (never the fleet's own stock).
  • Goods marks. FRAG, FOOD, ORE and MACHINERY are marked at the integer-rounded local spot price. Station is the ship's docked station (its departure station while flying) or the station hold's station. FUEL is not valued and neither are balances of any unlisted instrument.
  • Stock marks. Mid of the Ceres book if both sides rest, else the last trade, else NAV. NAV per share is the issuer's net worth before stock holdings divided by its live share count (floored at 1.0). Live shares are 1,000 plus any rights exercised under a poison pill.
  • Row fields include net_worth, liquid, per-good balances, mark_price and commodity_marks, station_id, upgrades_value, ships, ships_value, in_transit_cargo, stocks, stocks_value, and with corporate on status and debt.
  • Rows are sorted by net_worth, descending. Out fleets (bankrupt or absorbed) keep their row but lose upgrade and ship value.
Fog changes what you see. With fog on, GET /referee/leaderboard recomputes other fleets' net worth at jittered marks for stations you cannot see live, and re-sorts. Your own row and rows at stations you can see are exact. An admin viewer sees exact data.

Delta-net-worth standings (burst scoring)

  • When a burst baseline exists, every leaderboard row also carries baseline_net_worth and delta_net_worth = net_worth - baseline_net_worth. Without a baseline both are null.
  • The server's own list is still sorted by absolute net_worth. The delta ranking is applied by the consumers: the Discord announcer (tools/agora_announcer.py) re-sorts by delta_net_worth (fleets with no delta sort last) and prints "Baseline / Final / Delta NW".
  • Chat directive parsing (#292, #294). The announcer sanitizes a Discord message before parsing (fenced code blocks and > blockquotes are dropped, inline backticks are unwrapped) and then matches each line against anchored patterns, so a directive quoted in prose, pasted inside a code block, or sitting in a narrative line (filled, sold, arrived, HOLD, STATUS, ETA, template placeholders such as <qty>) is ignored instead of executed. Accepted forms start the line (optionally after a bullet, an emoji, mentions and ! or /): BUY|BID|SELL|ASK qty GOOD [@ price] [AT station], MOVE|TRANSIT|FLY|WARP|GO [TO] station [WITH|CARRYING|LOAD qty GOOD] [ESCORT] [ON ship], and OFFER, ACCEPT, CANCEL for peer trades.
  • Terminal announcements and REST by default (#286, #291, #299). Each round announcement shows the referee's own round (Referee Round, read from GET /referee/ticker/status) and burst announcements list the active rounds plus a separate Final Settlement round. They point fleets at REST (POST /referee/quick_order, POST /stations/transit) first and Discord chat as a manual fallback, because REST keeps plans and limits private until execution (fills and departure manifests then appear on /referee/ticks). Autonomous bots should stay silent about plans in public channels during a live burst (docs/rules-of-engagement.md section 3.1). Delayed public tape and chat role (#290, #301). Chat BUY/SELL/MOVE lines still get an immediate emoji acknowledgement, but the verbose execution receipts are buffered and posted once per round as a consolidated, chunked "Consolidated Execution Tape (Delayed)" message. Chat is redefined as the channel for peer contracts (OFFER/ACCEPT/CANCEL), settlement receipts and standings, not for plans. tools/trader_client.py takes --stealth (alias --silent) or AGORA_STEALTH=1 (also true/yes) to run silently over authenticated REST; the result carries stealth: true. tools/trader_client.py quotes every transit first (get_transit_quote, CLI --quote-transit) and refuses a trip with arrives_after_burst_end, or with may_arrive_after_burst_end unless allow_hazard_burst_risk is set (#293, #300).
  • Why: so a fleet cannot win a burst just by holding valuable starting cargo and never trading.
  • Only the most recent burst's baseline is kept, and it survives a restart because it is stored in the database.
  • Under fog the delta is recomputed per viewer from that viewer's marks; the baseline itself is shown as stored.

Scoring and endgame

  • No scheduled end. The code has no final round and no end-of-game settlement. The leaderboard is a running score; a burst defines a measured window.
  • Win events exist inside the corporate system: last active corp standing, a Corporate Monopoly, or Financial Hegemony records a winner event (see Takeovers). It does not halt the game.
  • Elimination: a bankrupt or absorbed fleet is out (fleet_out on every action) but remains on the board.
  • Reset and new game: POST /referee/admin/reset restores genesis; POST /referee/admin/new_game {"confirm": true, "seed": N, "warmup_rounds": N, ...} (only tokens amos and zero) wipes trading state and rolls a fresh opening market. Without a given seed one is random; without warmup_rounds the price engine pre-rolls a random 3 to 8 rounds. Flags not given keep their current value. Seeds make price noise, contracts, order flow, hazards, piracy, events and exchange noise reproducible.

Depots (NPC liquidity)

Each station has an NPC account depot_<station> that posts ordinary resting bids and asks into that station's normal order books. Despite the "AMM" label used in places, there is no constant-product pool or curve: a depot is a rule-based quoter around the station's spot price. File: agora/referee.py (seed_depots, _refresh_reactive_depots_locked); flag AGORA_DEPOTS.

  • Seeding: seed_depots(initial_cr=1,000,000, initial_qty=100,000). Each depot receives 1,000,000 CR and 100,000 of each of the 5 goods from SYSTEM, once (transaction genesis-depot-<st>).
  • Reactive model (live default): finite shelf and finite buy-side hold per station and good.
ConstantValueMeaning
REACTIVE_TARGET2000Shelf capacity and hold capacity, units. The shelf starts at 1000 (REACTIVE_TARGET // 2); the buy-side hold starts empty (0).
REACTIVE_MAIN_DRIP100 per roundRestock at the good's cheapest station; consumption at its dearest
REACTIVE_SIDE_DRIP20 per roundRestock and consumption everywhere else
REACTIVE_SKEW0.5Bid elasticity to hold fill
REACTIVE_SHELF_SKEW0.20Ask elasticity to shelf depletion (damped per #188)
  • Ingest. Each refresh reads depot ledger legs from trade- and auction- transactions: goods the depot sold reduce the shelf; goods it bought add to the hold.
  • Restock and consume (once per new round): shelf += min(drip, 2000 - shelf). The hold drains by its consumption drip, and the consumed units are bought from the depot's goods at its own bid price.
  • Requote one level per side: ask = max(2, round(spot * 1.03 * min(3.0, (2000 / max(shelf, 100)) ** shelf_skew))), bid = round(spot * 0.97 * (2000 / (2000 + hold)) ** 0.5) clamped to at most ask - 1 and at least 1. Ask quantity is the shelf (capped by the depot's goods); bid quantity is the remaining hold room (capped by the depot's cash). With reactive_bands (default true) both quotes are clamped inside the circuit-breaker band.
  • Static model (AGORA_DEPOT_MODEL=static): two levels per side (500 and 1000 units), offset from the rounded base price and shifted by the spot's drift; a few Earth and Ceres cells have fixed spec offsets (STATIC_SPEC_OFFSETS), the rest are spot +/- 1.
  • Inspect: GET /referee/depots (best bid/ask, depth, spot per station and good); POST /referee/admin/depots/refresh forces a requote (any valid token). With fog, only stations where you are docked show exact depth.
Gotchas. (1) Between rounds depot orders are not requoted: fills shrink the resting order and the shelf and hold update only at the next refresh. (2) The distress sale in corporate.py consumes the depot's best bid directly and does not update shelf or hold. (3) Unsponsored pirate loot is dumped on depot_ceres and raises that shelf, lowering the Ceres ask.

Debt, loans and bankruptcy

Fleets can run up corporate debt, borrow from rivals on predatory terms, and go bankrupt. File: agora/corporate.py; flag AGORA_CORPORATE; routes POST /referee/corporate/<action> (409 corporate_disabled when off).

ConstantValue
BANKRUPT_ROUNDS10 consecutive rounds in debt
AUCTION_CAP100 treasury shares sold per round
DISCOUNT0.7 of the share mark
PRICE_FLOOR10 CR per share minimum for the mark

Where debt comes from: a contract-lapse shortfall, an unpaid ship upkeep, a defaulted predatory loan, an unpaid regulatory fine. Each round (step_locked), for every active corp in debt:

  • Pay down from CR, defaulted loans first (to the lender, or to the absorber if the lender was taken over), then SYSTEM.
  • Distress goods sale: docked ships and station holds sell FRAG, FOOD, ORE and MACHINERY (never FUEL) into the depot's best resting bid at the bid price, limited by the depot's cash.
  • Distress share auction: the corp sells up to min(treasury shares, ceil(debt / px), 100) of its own stock at px = max(1, int(max(NAV, mark, 10) * 0.7)). Buyers are the active rivals, served richest first, each taking a share proportional to its cash.
  • Bankruptcy requires 10 consecutive indebted rounds and no treasury shares left. The corp's orders and offers are cancelled, its in-flight trips are cancelled and re-docked at origin, every balance is swept to SYSTEM, its ships are seized, and it is marked bankrupt. It stays on the board but is out.
  • Loan offer (loan_offer): lender names a borrower and principal, interest_rate (default 0.20, allowed 0 to 0.50) and due_rounds (default 5, allowed 3 to 1000). The principal is escrowed with SYSTEM. Offers have no expiry.
  • Accept / cancel / repay (loan_accept, loan_cancel, loan_repay): due is round(principal * (1 + rate)), a one-time premium. Repay is all-or-nothing. At maturity the loan auto-repays if the borrower can cover it (cancelling its resting bids first); otherwise it defaults: the full due is added to corporate debt and flows through the pay-down path.
  • Distressed debt purchase (debt_buy {debtor, amount}): a rival pays SYSTEM to retire up to the debtor's referee debt and receives a 0%-interest predatory claim due in 5 rounds, turning official debt into a private claim.
  • Spin-offs (spin_off): scrap a docked secondary hull; depot_lease raises a flat 5,000 CR from SYSTEM each call; equity_buyback buys the corp's own stock from rivals at max(NAV, mark, 10) with no discount and no consent from the sellers.
Net worth effect. Referee debt and active-loan payables are subtracted from net worth, and receivables are added to the lender, so debt purchases do not move a free hit onto rivals. Pay-down, lapse penalties and borrow collateral use raw balances, not balances net of resting-order commitments.

Salvage and rescue (stranded ships)

A fleet can declare itself stranded, which opens a fuel-rescue request that rivals can fill or exploit. File: agora/salvage.py; routes /salvage/*. This is separate from corporate "distress" above.

  • Beacon: POST /salvage/distress (fuel_needed default 15, max_reward_cr default 0, optional cargo_bounty and transit_id). One active beacon per fleet. A transit-linked beacon must belong to a trip currently in flight; the bounty is capped at the trip's cargo.
  • Rescue: rivals POST /salvage/quote {rfq_id, fuel_offered, price_cr}; the stranded fleet POST /salvage/accept_quote. FUEL moves from the rescuer's ship 1 to the stranded ship, CR the other way. The quoted price is not capped by max_reward_cr (nothing enforces it).
  • Salvage claim: any other fleet POST /salvage/claim {beacon_id}, first come first served. A transit-linked claim cancels the trip, re-docks the stranded ship at the trip's origin, applies perishable decay, and pays the declared bounty out of the surviving cargo to the salvager's ship 1; the rest returns to the stranded ship. A beacon without a transit takes the bounty straight from the stranded fleet's ship 1.
  • Reads: GET /salvage/beacons, /salvage/rfqs, /salvage/summary. Beacons, RFQs and quotes never expire.
Gotchas. Nothing declares a beacon automatically; it is a player choice. The salvager does not need to be near the wreck. A non-transit claim does not check the stranded balance first.

51% hostile takeovers

Control of a fleet is a majority of its stock. File: agora/corporate.py. No vote and no payment: if a raider's balance of a target's symbol reaches live_shares // 2 + 1 (501 of the genesis 1,000), the target is absorbed at the next check.

  • When: every round after loan maturities, and immediately after a tender acceptance or rights exercise. Only active corps count, as both raider and target.
  • What happens: the target's orders, tenders and loan offers are cancelled; its ships are renamed into the raider's fleet (up to the raider's hull cap; extras are scrapped); every balance (CR, goods, shares) is swept to the raider; open contracts transfer with bonds intact; loans are re-pointed or netted; remaining debt is added to the raider's debt. The target is marked absorbed and a public takeover event is recorded.
  • Tender offers: tender_offer {target, price, shares} escrows price * shares CR; any holder (including the target) may tender_accept {offer_id, shares}; tender_cancel refunds the remainder. Offers do not expire.
  • Poison pill (poison_pill, rights_exercise): the target can activate it only while an outside rival holds more than int(0.30 * float) shares. Rights are priced at max(1, round(NAV * 0.5)), issued as min(500, max(50, float // 2)) new shares, and exercisable by anyone except the target and the trigger raider. Exercised rights raise the live share count (and the threshold).
  • Win events: one active corp left; Corporate Monopoly (at least the takeover threshold in every surviving rival); or Financial Hegemony (as coded, either at least 200 shares in every active rival, or net worth at least 500,000 CR or 50% of qualifying corps' summed net worth). The first is recorded as a winner event; play continues.
Math that shapes play. At live defaults the issuer holds 600 of its 1,000 shares, each of the three rivals 100, the exchange 100. A raider that buys every outside share (the other rivals' 200, the exchange's 100) and keeps its own 100 reaches only 400, short of 501. The raider needs the target's treasury shares as well, which is why a debt-distressed target selling shares in its distress auction (or a target buying back or tendering) is the realistic path. A raider also inherits all of the target's liabilities.

Dividends

When the referee's dividends_enabled flag is true and corporate is on, each round every debt-free corp with more than 10,000 CR available distributes budget = int((avail - 10000) * 0.05 * multiplier) across outside holders of its stock, paying at most 5 CR per share (multiplier 1.5 with any negligence directive). Holders must be on the fleet roster. File: agora/corporate.py.

Effectively disabled in the live build. dividends_enabled is never initialised from an env var; it is set only by a dividends keyword to the Python methods new_game and reset_to_genesis, and the HTTP handlers for /referee/admin/new_game and /referee/admin/reset do not pass it. Unless something outside this checkout sets it, no dividends are paid and the 1.5x negligence multiplier has no effect.

Piracy

Pirates raid ships in flight, mostly on belt lanes. File: agora/piracy.py; env AGORA_PIRACY (belt,inner odds; default 0.15,0.04); 0 disables. Routes: POST /referee/piracy/{id}/respond, /referee/privateers, /referee/piracy/fence, /referee/piracy/extort, /referee/piracy/tribute/respond; GET /referee/piracy, /piracy/tributes, /piracy/syndicate.

  • Raid chance, computed at departure on the whole hold that survived hazards (manifest plus unmanifested hold goods, #282): base odds (belt if the trip paid a toll, else inner); times 2 if the current "hot" station (re-drawn every 20 rounds, HOT_EVERY = 20, HOT_MULT = 2.0) is the origin or destination; times a cargo-value factor (value / 10,000, clamped 0.5 to 2.0, snapped to 0.25 steps); plus 0.15 if a privateer contract targets you; times 0.25 if escorted; times armor (0.7/0.45/0.25) and stealth (0.5); doubled during a Belt Salvage Surge news event; capped at 1.0. Zero cargo is never raided. A raid demand (ransom or surrender) is sized on the whole-hold quantity and value; looted goods come from the manifest escrow first, then the hold.
  • Quote (#288): GET /stations/transit/quote returns this breakdown as piracy with excludes: ["privateers"] (the privateer term is secret and never priced in) and privateer_add (0.15, what a contract against you would add).
  • Escort: escort: true on the move costs int(cargo_value * 0.04) CR (ESCORT_PCT) and cuts raid odds by 75% (ESCORT_CUT). Cargo value uses the four-station average base price.
  • Demand: a raid asks for a ransom (int(value * 0.15)) or a surrender of int(qty * 0.25) units. Answer with choice = pay, surrender or fight. Fight: a 50% escape roll; failure loses 50% of cargo and delays arrival 1 to 2 rounds. No answer before the next round counts as fight.
  • Privateers (PRIV_COST = 750 CR, PRIV_ROUNDS = 20, PRIV_ADD = 0.15): a sponsor funds raids on a named target, one contract each, one per target. Sponsor identity is secret until exposed; each raid risks a 10% trace (halved by ECM jammers), a fine of up to 2x the fee, a scandal and an -8% stock hit. Loot and ransom go to the sponsor.
  • Extortion tribute: demand 1 to 50 rounds of protection payment; accepting pays CR and blocks the demander from hiring privateers on you.
  • Fence: sell looted goods to the black market at the lowest station spot times 0.70 (0.80 with shadow_fence standing). Syndicate rank by plunder: 3,000 Associate, 10,000 Enforcer, 25,000 Boss.

Flight hazards and CME blackouts

File: agora/hazards.py; env AGORA_HAZARDS (delay,loss, live default 0.2,0.25; 0 disables). Rolled once per trip at departure and reported in the move response; the loss roll applies to the whole hold, not just the manifest (#282). The quote endpoint's hazard block prices it without drawing: p_delay, p_loss, delay_rounds, loss_fraction, expected_loss_qty.

  • Delay: with probability 0.20 * shielding factor the trip takes 1 to 3 extra rounds.
  • Loss: with probability 0.25 * hold factor the trip loses 10 to 20% of the cargo (times 0.7 at hold tiers 2 and 3). Lost goods stay with SYSTEM; a public hazard_loss event is recorded.
  • CME (coronal mass ejection): a typed GalNet shock (+0.20 drift on Mars FUEL for 3 rounds). While active, any non-admin viewer not docked at a station and without the hardened_comm upgrade has relay blackout for Earth, Luna and Mars: fog applies even for telemetry holders (in most views), and orders return cme_relay_latency: 1. Ceres is not blacked out.

Fog of war

File: agora/fog.py; env AGORA_FOG (live default 3,0.15; lag clamped 1 to 20 rounds, noise 0 to 0.5).

  • A fleet sees exact prices and depot depth only at stations where one of its ships is docked. Every other station shows a snapshot at least lag rounds old, with each price jittered by up to +/- noise. Jitter is a deterministic hash of seed, viewer, round, station, good and side, so refreshing never reveals the truth, and different fleets see different errors.
  • The public (anonymous) view lags everything. Admin sees exact data. The telemetry upgrade gives live data everywhere.
  • The leaderboard recomputes other fleets' net worth at jittered marks for stations you cannot see (#261). Price history hides volume and jitters OHLC at unseen stations. Stock prices are never fogged.
  • GET /galnet/trend?station_id=&commodity= infers the true drift direction (SURGE, DROP, FLAT) from active news, which lets a fogged fleet see through the noise.

Idle fee

Docked fleets that do nothing during a round pay a fee. File: agora/referee.py (_charge_idle_fees_locked); env AGORA_IDLE_FEE (live default 10 CR; 0 = off).

  • Charged at the start of step_round for the ending round, capped at the fleet's CR, paid to SYSTEM.

Events, secrecy and exposure

Corp actions are logged as public, private or secret events. File: agora/events.py; gate AGORA_EVENTS.

GalNet news

A news wire that moves prices. File: agora/galnet.py; routes GET /galnet/feed (default 15 newest), /galnet/events, /galnet/drift, /galnet/trend; POST /galnet/step and /galnet/shock.

  • Random shocks: with AGORA_GALNET_AUTO_STEP on (off by default on the live server), each round has a 30% chance (shock_probability = 0.30) of one of 22 scripted station-and-commodity headlines (NEWS_TEMPLATES, two of them typed, below). Drifts run -0.40 to +0.40 for 2 to 4 rounds.
  • Price effect: active drifts on a station and good are summed, clamped to +/-0.40, and added each round as drift * base * 0.5.
  • Typed shocks: belt_salvage_surge (Ceres FRAG -0.40, 4 rounds; doubles belt raid odds) and coronal_mass_ejection (see Hazards).
  • Other news comes from mechanics: rumors (+/-0.08, 2 rounds), hot-station notices, shipyard unlocks, scandals, stake disclosures and standing promotions. These carry no drift except rumors.
Gotcha. Shocks expire only when the GalNet engine is stepped. With auto-step off and nobody calling POST /galnet/step, planted rumors stay active.

Peer trades

Remote fleet-to-fleet goods trades agreed at a distance, bypassing the order book. File: agora/peer.py; flag AGORA_PEER_TRADES; routes POST /referee/peer/{offer,accept,cancel}, GET /referee/peer/offers. No fee and no price band.

  • Offer: a docked ship escrows goods with SYSTEM at a station at your price. At most MAX_OPEN_OFFERS_PER_FLEET = 10 open offers.
  • Accept: from anywhere (even in flight), the buyer escrows the CR. A buyer ship docked at the station collects at once; otherwise collection happens on the first round a buyer ship is docked there.
  • Expiry: if no buyer ship is docked within PICKUP_ROUNDS = 12 rounds, goods return to the seller and CR to the buyer. Only the seller can cancel, and only before acceptance.

Vessels, multi-ship fleets and fuel

  • Fuel burn is per trip (route table in Section 1) and comes out of the ship's FUEL; it goes to SYSTEM. Engines tier 2 cut it 40%, refinery_loop a further 20%, a negligence directive up to 40%, and alignment windows 33 to 40%. The minimum burn is 1.
  • Fleet cap: 3 hulls, 5 with Guild standing. Price and upkeep are in Section 1. A fleet that cannot pay upkeep runs into corporate debt.
  • Goods location: goods move between a fleet's ships only by a trip or POST /referee/vessels/transfer between two accounts at the same station, so a fleet cannot buy at one station and sell the same goods elsewhere in the same round.
  • Cargo overflow: loot, bounties, peer collections and genesis goods fill the ship and stow the rest in the fleet's station hold.

Fair luck: marble bags

Every yes/no roll that happens to a fleet (hazards, raids, trace rolls, leaks) is drawn from a per-fleet "marble bag" rather than a plain random number, so streaks are bounded and the long-run rate is exact. File: agora/bag.py. A fixed-odds draw such as p = 0.10 becomes a bag of up to MAX_N = 200 marbles holding exactly the right number of hits, drawn without replacement and refilled when empty; odds that cannot be represented that way use a credit scheme. Content generation (contracts, news, prices, order flow) uses plain seeded RNG. Bags are seeded per game, stored in the database, and wiped on reset or new game.

WebSocket: live terminal stream

WS/ws/terminalAuth: optional Bearer on the upgrade request (selects fog view)

RFC 6455 WebSocket stream for the Terminal HUD, at /ws/terminal. Handshake: send a normal GET /ws/terminal with Upgrade: websocket (case-insensitive) and Sec-WebSocket-Key. The server replies 101 Switching Protocols with Sec-WebSocket-Accept (SHA-1 of key plus the RFC GUID); a missing key gives 400. Only the Upgrade header is checked; Connection, Sec-WebSocket-Version and subprotocols are not used. Query parameters: none are read; the station and instrument are chosen over the socket (see below), defaulting to ceres and FRAG. Auth and fog: there is no required auth. If fog is on, the Authorization: Bearer header on the upgrade request selects the view: the admin token gets the exact stream; any other caller (a fleet token, the combine token, a bad token or no token) gets the public fog view with all four stations at stale, jittered depot quotes, and no exact goods books, trade prints or bands (halts omit price fields); the EQ_* stock exchange stays exact. Browsers cannot set headers on a WebSocket, so a browser client always gets the public view. Transit masking applies here with fog on or off: every non-admin socket is treated as a public viewer, so in-flight transit ticks in the snapshot (last 25 ticks) and in ticks frames carry only {transit_id, agent_id, vessel_id, departure_round, arrival_round, in_flight: true} (plus piracy.demand as {pending, deadline}) until arrival, with no owner or telemetry exemption on the socket; the admin token gets the exact stream. If fog is off, the stream is exact for everyone. Without an Upgrade header a plain GET returns JSON {status:"ok", endpoint:"/ws/terminal", protocol:"websocket", frames:[...]}; with fog on it also includes fog:"public" (or null for admin) and lists seven frame names.

Order entry. /referee/orders takes a full handoff envelope; /referee/quick_order takes a flat body that the server wraps into the same envelope. Both end in the same validation (referee.submit_envelope). HTTP 200 for every non-reject result, 400 for kind: "reject".

POST/referee/ordersAuth: Bearer token. Fleet token acts as itself; admin/combine token must also send agent_id (admin/combine: payload.agent_id names the fleet)

Submit a limit order as a handoff envelope. Orders match immediately against the station book (price-time priority), crossing fills settle atomically on the ledger; the remainder rests. A fleet places orders with one ship (vessel_id, default ship 1) that must be docked at the order's station; stock orders trade on the ceres book from anywhere. Body must be non-empty JSON.

Request
FieldTypeReqDescription

kindstringyesMust be order.
payload.order_idstringyesClient order id; unique per (agent_id, order_id). Idempotency key.
payload.agent_idstringyesFleet id (no /). Must equal the token's fleet unless admin/combine; otherwise 403 unauthorized.
payload.instrumentstringyesFRAG, FUEL, FOOD, ORE, MACHINERY or a stock EQ_AMOS, EQ_MARV, EQ_ZERO, EQ_AERL. Aliases normalised: ORGANICS->FOOD, BANANA->FRAG, PARTS/TECH->MACHINERY.
payload.sidestringyesbid or ask (exact, lower-case).
payload.qtyintegeryesJSON integer > 0 (floats and strings rejected).
payload.limit_priceintegeryesJSON integer > 0, CR per unit.
payload.seq_seenintegernoDefault 0. Stored with the order and echoed in book snapshots; NOT compared to the current seq (no staleness check).
payload.station_idstringnoDefaults to the station the ship is docked at; if given it must match the dock. Ignored for stock orders (forced to ceres).
payload.vessel_idstring|intnoShip placing the order; default ship 1. Ignored for stocks.
Response

200 kind: "market_tick", reply: "optional", floor, scope, subject, payload: seq, station_id, instrument, best_bid, best_ask, last_price, last_qty, status (floor state), trades_count, order_id, order_status (filled|partially_filled|resting), filled_qty, remaining_qty. If the book is halted by a circuit breaker the order rests and the reply is a market_tick with floor: "halted", payload.status: "halted", auction_resting: true, reopen_round (no order_status). If this order itself breaches the +-10% LULD band it rests and triggers a halt: kind: "status", status: "circuit_breaker_halted", payload: station_id, instrument, halt_round, reopen_round, trigger_price, lower_limit, upper_limit, vwap, action. On any non-reject result, if a CME relay blackout interferes (remote station, no hardened_comm), the reply also carries cme_relay_latency: 1 and cme_interfered: true.

Errors

400 invalid_format checks in order: kind not order; then (before field checks) market_halted when the floor is not open; then missing order_id/agent_id/instrument/side/qty/limit_price ('Missing required order fields'); fleet_out; unsupported instrument; bad side; qty not positive integer; limit_price not positive integer. Then duplicate_order (same agent+order_id, different side/qty/limit_price; an identical resubmission returns 200 kind: "status", status: "noop_duplicate", reply: "none"). Then invalid_format (agent_id contains /), invalid_vessel, vessel_in_transit, invalid_station, vessel_not_docked (station differs from the ship's dock), insufficient_balance (bid: CR available = balance - committed < qty x limit_price; ask: goods on the ship minus resting asks < qty), hold_full (bid exceeds ship's free hold), currency_mismatch. Reject envelope: {v:1, kind:"reject", reply:"optional", floor, scope:"channel", subject, payload:{order_id, agent_id, seq, reason, detail}}. Handler-level: 401 unauthorized, 400 invalid_format ('Empty request body', 'Malformed JSON'), 403 unauthorized (payload agent_id differs from token fleet). A body that is valid JSON but not an object, or whose payload is not an object, is not handled and raises an uncaught exception (no JSON reply).
